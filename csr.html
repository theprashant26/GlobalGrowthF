A note on these figures

Every value on this page is a labelled placeholder pending verification. We will publish each one only when it has been counted and can be defended, and we will publish the years where a number falls rather than quietly dropping the metric.

Where Section 135 of the Companies Act applies to the group, our statutory CSR obligation is treated as a floor and a compliance matter — not as the definition of this programme.
