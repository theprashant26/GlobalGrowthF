Our Story

Why twenty-seven divisions is a group, not a portfolio

Diversification usually means chasing whatever is growing. We built the opposite way, and it shows in how the divisions depend on each other.

Skilling came first, because every other business we wanted to run needed trained people and the market could not supply them at the standard we required. That division now staffs our security and driver operations, and sells its training to the wider market on the same terms.

Logistics followed, because moving goods is the business that teaches an organisation to be honest about time. Engineering and electrical work came next and fed manufacturing, which supplies the infrastructure projects. Technology was built internally to schedule the fleets and track the warehouses long before it was offered to a client.

The result is a group where the handovers are ours. When a consignment moves from our warehouse to our truck to our last mile, there is no third party to point at when it goes wrong — and that is the whole argument for building it this way.

05 / Governance

Governance

One standard, regardless of division

A group operating across fifteen sectors can either run fifteen sets of rules or one. We run one, and the compliance function reports at group level rather than into the businesses it has to say no to.

    On the sectors marked as planned

    Banking, Finance, Insurance and Pharmacy are licensed activities in India, regulated by the Reserve Bank of India, SEBI, IRDAI and the State Pharmacy Council respectively. They appear on this site because they are part of our stated direction and we would rather be transparent about that than quiet.

    They are not operational, are not offered, and carry no enquiry route. That will change only when the applicable licence or registration is in force — and the badge will come off the same day, not before.
