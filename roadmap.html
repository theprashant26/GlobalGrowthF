Growth Roadmap

Three phases, in a deliberate order

Cash-generating services first. Asset-backed businesses second. Licensed and capital-intensive sectors last — and only once the balance sheet, the standing and the approvals are genuinely in place.

01 / Sequence

Why This Order

Most groups fail by entering the last phase first

Aviation, energy and financial services are the sectors that make a group look serious. They are also the ones that require a balance sheet, a compliance record and a regulator's confidence — none of which can be bought at the point of entry.

So we sequenced it the other way. Phase 1 businesses were chosen because they generate cash quickly, employ people at scale and expose an operator's discipline immediately — a late delivery or an untrained guard is visible the same week. Phase 2 converts that operating record into assets. Phase 3 is where the group spends the credibility the first two phases earned.

It is a slower route. It is also the reason this site marks some sectors as planned rather than presenting the whole architecture as though it were already running.

03 / Criteria

Entry Criteria

What has to be true before we enter a sector

A sector moves from planned to operational when all four of these hold. Three out of four is not a start date.

It strengthens something we run

A new division has to make an existing one better — as a supplier, a customer or a shared capability. A business that only sits alongside the others is a distraction.

We can staff it to standard

If our own skilling division cannot produce the people, or the market cannot supply them at the standard the work requires, we are not ready — regardless of demand.

The licence is in force

Where an activity is regulated, the authorisation must be granted and current. Not applied for, not expected — granted. This is the criterion that holds Phase 3 back.

We can fund it to steady state

Capital has to cover the business to the point it stands on its own, not just to launch. Entering a capital-intensive sector underfunded damages every other division.
