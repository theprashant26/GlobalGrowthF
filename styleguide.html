03 — Spacing

08 — Navigation

Navbar, mega-menu and drawer

The header at the top of this page is the real component, rendered from nav.js and sectors.js. Scroll down and it solidifies to navy with a blur past 80px.

To review it: hover Our Sectors in the header for the three-column mega-menu of all 15 sectors, and Divisions for the 12 with dedicated pages. Narrow the window below 1100px for the full-screen drawer with its staggered reveal. Tab through the header to confirm the menus open on keyboard, close on Esc, and that focus is trapped inside the open drawer.
Behaviour contract
  • Transparent over a dark hero; .is-solid past 80px. A page with no dark hero adds .gg-nav--solid to start solid.
  • Mega-menu opens on hover for a fine pointer, on click for touch and keyboard, and closes on Esc or focus leaving the item.
  • Planned sectors appear in the menu de-emphasised and labelled, never as a plain link.
  • The drawer traps focus, locks background scroll, returns focus to the toggle on close, and closes itself if the viewport widens past the desktop breakpoint.

11 — Chrome

Preloader, progress, cursor, back-to-top

Four pieces of site furniture. All four are removed entirely — not merely stilled — under prefers-reduced-motion.

Preloader

Shown once per session and hard-capped at 1.8 seconds, with a backstop timer so a stalled asset can never leave a visitor staring at an overlay. Bounded here so it can be inspected; in production it is full-screen and wipes upward to reveal the hero.

068

Scroll progress

Three pixels of accent gradient, fixed to the very top of the viewport. The live one is running at the top of this page — scroll and watch it fill.

Custom cursor

A dot that tracks the pointer exactly and a ring that lags behind it, scaling up over interactive elements. Live on this page if you are on a desktop with a mouse; never created at all on a touch device.

Back to top

Appears after 60% of a viewport height of scrolling. The live one is bottom-right of this page.
