# Vacancy notices — moved

The list of posts still awaiting a notice is now a designed page:

**`/approvals.html#vacancies`**

On the review build: <https://theprashant26.github.io/GlobalGrowthF/approvals.html#vacancies>

It is generated from `assets/js/data/jobs.js` at render time rather than written
out here, so it cannot drift: a post gains a `vacancies` value and it disappears
from the page on the next load. Nothing needs regenerating by hand.

Planned divisions — Banking, Pharmacy, Finance and Insurance — are excluded from
that page deliberately. They must not carry a vacancy or an application fee
until the relevant licence is in force.
