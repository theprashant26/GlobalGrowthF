01 / Culture

People who would rather run an operation than describe one.

Why Work Here

Honest about what this is

This is not a settled corporate with a decade of process behind it. If that is what you want, we are the wrong employer and we would rather say so now than three months in.

What we do have is scope, and a structure written down before it was needed. Twenty-seven divisions are being built, systems are being written and standards are being set. The people who joined earliest are running things they would have waited years for elsewhere — not because we are generous, but because somebody had to and they put their hand up.

It also means the work is real and visible. You will not spend a quarter on a deck. You will spend it on a site, in a warehouse, or in a system that fifty people depend on from Monday.
