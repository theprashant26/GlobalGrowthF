# Vacancy notices — what is still needed

**126 posts across 20 divisions.** Four divisions are done: Aviation, Metro,
Hotels, and two of Healthcare's seven.

Banking, Pharmacy, Finance and Insurance are **excluded on purpose** — they are
planned divisions awaiting regulatory approval and must not carry vacancies or
fees until licensed.

---

## Why we cannot fill these in for you

Two fields in the tables below are not copy. They are commercial terms of a
transaction with the public, and nobody on the build team can invent them.

**Vacancies.** This number tells a real person a job exists. Advertising 200
posts where there are 20 is not a content decision.

**Application fee.** This is money taken from job-seekers. Across 126 posts at
the rates already set, the difference between a guessed number and the real one
runs into lakhs — collected from people who cannot easily spare ₹250.

Everything else in the tables we can draft from your existing structure if you
would rather review than write. Say the word.

---

## What each post needs

Copy the format you used for Aviation. Per post:

| Field | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Job code** | `GGIPL-AVI-TKT-003` | `GGIPL` – division – function – serial |
| **Vacancies** | `240` | Posts actually being recruited |
| **Application fee** | `₹296` | Per application |
| **Qualification** | `12th pass / Intermediate or Graduate…` | Full sentence |
| **Experience** | `0–3 years in airline ticketing…` | Full sentence; say if freshers may apply |
| **Job description** | One paragraph | What the role is responsible for |
| **Key responsibilities** | 8–14 bullets | We publish all of them, unedited |

**Salary, grade and required documents are already in the build** and only need
changing if they have moved. Note that Aviation's salaries rose sharply between
your September structure and these notices — tell us if the same applies
elsewhere.

**Send in batches of three or four divisions.** A single message will hit the
50,000-character limit, which is what truncated the last one part-way through
Healthcare's third role.

## One thing worth deciding before the rest arrive

At the four divisions already published, **4,140 posts are advertised**. The
remaining 20 divisions are broadly the same size, so the finished total will
land somewhere near **25,000–30,000 posts**, each carrying a ₹99–₹299 fee.

That is a large number to stand behind. It is worth confirming with Ashwini
Kumar that the licence and the sign-off were given against a figure of that
order, and not against the four divisions published so far.

If some of these are indicative headcount plans rather than posts being
recruited now, they should not be published as vacancies with fees attached.
There is a clean way to show both: live notices for what is actually open, and
the existing position structure for the rest. The site already does exactly
that — an open notice shows a post count and a fee, and everything else shows
as structure.
