Planned and regulated activities

This is the most important notice on this page. Several sectors and divisions shown on this website are marked “Planned — subject to regulatory approval”. Those activities are not operational. They include banking, finance, payments, investment, insurance, pharmacy, pharmaceuticals and aviation ground handling.

Each requires prior authorisation from the relevant statutory authority — the Reserve Bank of India, SEBI, IRDAI, the State Pharmacy Council, the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation or the Directorate General of Civil Aviation, as applicable. Global Growth Industries Private Limited does not solicit, offer, accept deposits for, or carry on any such activity, and will not do so unless and until the applicable licence or registration is in force.

They appear on this site to be transparent about the group's stated direction. They carry no enquiry route and no call to action for that reason. Nothing on this site should be read as an offer or solicitation in respect of any of them.
