This policy explains what this website collects, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

This site collects information only when you choose to send it. That means the fields you complete on the contact form (name, organisation, email, telephone, the department you select and your message) and on the careers form (the same fields plus your years of experience, the role applied for and the CV file you attach).

We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this site, and we do not embed third-party content that would set cookies on your device. Enquiries are used to respond to you and are routed to the department you selected. Applications are used to assess you for the role you applied for and for similar roles, which is what the consent checkbox on that form covers.
